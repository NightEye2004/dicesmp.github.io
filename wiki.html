HOW THINGS WORK

Server features

Helpful extras that keep the focus on Minecraft and the community.

⌂Claims & protectionKeep your builds yours+

Use a golden shovel to select two corners and create a protected claim. You can own up to 4 separate claims. Claim blocks start at 100, accrue at 100 per hour played, and cap at 80,000; 5 vote points buy 100 more in /voteshop. Unused claims expire after 14 days, and all claims expire after 60 days of inactivity except for players with a large claim-block balance. Creepers and TNT won’t damage the surface or claimed land.

♧TeamsBuild and adventure together+

Teams start at 10 players, 10 team chests, and 1 team warp. Upgrading to level 2 costs 20 score and raises the limits to 20 players, 20 chests, and 2 warps. ClanWar adds organized 5v5 matches with a 60-second preparation phase, a 30-second kit vote, and spectator mode.

✦Chests & tradingOrganize, stock, and sell+

Shopkeepers let you set up item-for-item trades using a hired Wandering Trader and a linked container. Hiring costs 32 emeralds, each trader links to one container within 15 blocks, and there is no per-player shop limit. See the illustrated setup below.

⚄Diamonds, votes & rewardsSupport the server and earn rewards+

Diamonds are used for player trading, the CoinFlip game, and the diamond bank. Each vote gives 1 vote point and 1 Vote Crate key—not a diamond. Spend 5 points for 100 claim blocks in /voteshop, then open your Vote Crates at /warp crates; their 15 rewards include iron gear, food, diamonds, and a Shopkeeper. ChatGames award a base 1 diamond, with a chance at bonus diamonds.

☁Player warps & teleportingMeet up with other players+

Essentials handles /home, /tpa, and /tpahere. Homes start at 3 and increase with rank up to 9. Player warps are public destinations: each player can create up to 3 with /setwarp and remove them with /delwarp. Browse them with /warps; warps have a 3-second delay, a 10-second cooldown, and cancel if you move.

☾Death chests & recoveryKnow what is and isn’t saved+

AngelChest protects your items and collects your experience. A chest lasts 10,000 seconds, and you can have up to 5 at once; when a sixth is created, the oldest chest drops its contents. PvP death chests are protected. Use /actp to teleport to a chest, /aclist to list them, or /acfetch to fetch one. If a server issue caused your death, staff may restore lost items; deaths caused by player error aren’t eligible for server-related restoration.

⟳Restart reminders & updatesStay in the loop in-game+

The in-game changelog keeps server updates available right in Minecraft, so you don’t need to visit Discord or the website every time something changes. On supported newer Minecraft versions, updates appear through a custom screen/dialog. DiceCustomPlugin also announces scheduled restarts up to 24 hours ahead and shows a boss-bar countdown as restart time approaches.

◉Voice, chat & custom looksMore ways to hang out+

DiscordSRV syncs in-game chat with Discord; use /discord link to connect your account. InteractiveChat supports item, inventory, Ender Chest, and ping tags in chat; its Discord add-on brings supported previews to Discord too. Voice chat is Java-only. ItemsAdder adds custom textures, items, and cosmetics.

◇Crossplay & versionsJava and Bedrock together+

Geyser and Floodgate let Bedrock players join the Java server; gameplay mechanics follow Java. ViaVersion and ViaBackwards support other client versions, but items or textures may look different when your game version is older or newer than the server. For best results, Java players should use 1.21.11 and Bedrock players the latest version.

♢Other handy detailsSmall things worth knowing+

Clumps combines nearby experience orbs into fewer, larger orbs to make XP collection smoother and reduce the number of entities on the ground. Simple_RTP sends you up to 30,000 blocks from spawn in the Overworld, with a 160-second cooldown and a 5-second countdown; random teleport is disabled in the Nether and End. SkinsRestorer lets players using offline-mode clients display custom skins. CoreProtect keeps activity logs for staff investigations. nLogin adds password authentication because the server runs in offline mode: Java players use in-game dialogs to create a password on first join and sign in when they return, while Bedrock players are signed in automatically through Floodgate. Balloons from DiceCustomPlugin follow your character around.

Visit /warp afk to enter the AFK zone. Every 30 seconds you get 3 reward rolls; each roll has its own chance. /afk is the normal AFK toggle, not the reward zone.

PLAYER-TO-PLAYER SHOPS

Set up a Shopkeeper

Make an item-for-item shop using a Wandering Trader and a container.

  1. 01

    Hire a Wandering Trader

    Hold 32 emeralds and shift-right-click a Wandering Trader to hire them. Place a chest, double chest, or barrel for the shop’s stock.

  2. 02

    Link the stock container

    Hold the trader egg and right-click the container you placed. Then place the trader where you want them to stand; keep the container within 15 blocks. Each trader can link to one container, and there is no per-player shop limit.

  3. 03

    Stock items and set the trade

    Put the items you want to sell into the linked container. Shift-right-click your trader to open the editor. Set the Result (what the customer receives), Cost 1 (the required payment), and optional Cost 2. Click item stacks to adjust trade quantities, up to 64. You need at least one item for each trade slot you configure.

THE OFFICIAL RULES

Minecraft server rules

These are the official DiceSMP rules. Please read them before playing.

!

Immediate IP ban: Breaking any rule below results in an automatic IP ban. The server uses automated logging, and the owner states there are no exceptions or warnings.

01

Server & community

Help keep the community kind, friendly, and peaceful.

  • Respect each other. Treat all players with kindness. Bullying, harassment, and toxic behavior are not tolerated.
  • No below-the-belt jokes. Keep chat friendly. Offensive, highly personal, or deeply hurtful jokes are prohibited.
  • No drama. Keep personal arguments out of global chat so the community stays peaceful and fun.
02

In-game gameplay

Play fairly and don’t use traps or exploits against other players.

  • No cheating. Modded clients, X-ray packs, hacked clients, and unapproved third-party modifications are forbidden.
  • No duping. Do not exploit game mechanics or glitches to duplicate items, blocks, or currency.
  • No spam killing. Do not repeatedly kill the same player to ruin their experience or farm them.
  • No TP trapping. Do not set traps that instantly kill players who accept a teleport request using /tpa.
  • No AngelChest trapping. Do not trap another player’s AngelChest or build around or underneath it to steal their gear or kill them when they retrieve it.
  • No pWarp voids. Do not set a player warp with /pwarp that sends players directly into the void, lava, or a fatal drop.
03

Performance & farm restrictions

Build responsibly and protect server performance.

  • No overpowered farms. Massive, unrestricted automated farms that consume excessive server resources are not allowed.
  • No iron farms. Traditional and villager-based iron golem farms are prohibited.
  • No lag machines. Redstone loops, entity crammers, or other contraptions designed to reduce server TPS are prohibited and result in a permanent ban.

For reports, concerns, or questions, open a ticket in the DiceSMP Discord ↗.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Still stuck? The Discord ticket team can help.

Can Java and Bedrock players play together?+

Yes. Join play.dicesmp.online. Bedrock uses port 12011. Server mechanics are based on Java Edition.

How many claims and homes can I have?+

You can own up to 4 separate land claims in the Overworld. Homes start at 3 and increase with rank: Copper 4, Gold 6, and Emerald 9.

Do the Nether and End have land claims?+

No. GriefPrevention claims are enabled in the Overworld only. The Nether and End are open exploration without land claims.

How do I rank up?+

Open /rankup in game and meet the lifetime ore, vote, and player-kill requirements for your next rank. The path runs from Coal to Netherite and also unlocks more homes and kits.

What do I get for voting?+

Each vote gives 1 vote point and 1 Vote Crate key. Spend 5 points for 100 claim blocks in /voteshop, then use your keys at /warp crates.

Does AngelChest keep my experience?+

Yes. AngelChest stores your items and collects your experience. A chest lasts 10,000 seconds, and you can have up to 5 at once.

How do I report a player or a server problem?+

Open a ticket in the DiceSMP Discord for reports, concerns, bugs, or suggestions.

What if I died because of a server issue?+

Contact staff through a Discord ticket. Staff can restore items when the death was caused by a server issue, not player error.
